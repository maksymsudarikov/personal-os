# Decisions Log

Append-only record of meaningful decisions and why they were made. Add entries manually or ask your OS to log them for you.

**Format:**

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

**Decision:** what was decided.
**Why:** the reasoning and constraints.
**Alternatives considered:** what else was on the table.
```

Keep it terse. The *why* is what matters — the *what* is in the code or outcome.
